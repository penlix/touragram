Piazza Navona, Rome

You're standing on what was once the Stadium of Domitian, commissioned in 86 AD for athletic contests. The piazza you see today preserves the exact oval shape of the ancient track beneath your feet.

At the centre, Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers, completed in 1651, places allegorical figures of the Nile, Ganges, Danube and Río de la Plata around an Egyptian obelisk, each one representing a continent known to the baroque world.
